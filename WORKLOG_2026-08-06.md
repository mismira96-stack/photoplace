## Why Things Felt Risky Today

- The original issue was overseas history, but fixes touched multiple cross-cutting paths.
- `MainActivity.java` is still doing UI, state, MediaStore, navigation, history, and cleanup work together.
- Performance regressions came from correct-but-heavy validation being run near home/history rendering.
- Old physical folders and new canonical names are a migration problem, not just a naming problem.
