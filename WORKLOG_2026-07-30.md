## Important Observations

- Google Photos place labels such as "Everland" or "Seoul Arts Center" are not necessarily the same data returned by Android `Geocoder`.
- Do not assume a Google Photos-visible POI can be reliably reproduced by the app.
- Broad POI promotion remains risky. Use Personal Place recommendations for user-confirmed naming instead.
- Fold opened layouts can expose spacing/cropping issues that normal phones hide.
- Home is acceptable now, but first-draw performance must remain a regression watch item.
